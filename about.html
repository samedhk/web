About Me

I am an engineer and scientist with a strong foundation in signal processing. My academic and research journey in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology, with a specialization in avionics, has been guided by a rigorous appreciation for mathematics and its application in information theory and control systems.

My technical experience spans LiDAR, radar, control systems, and image processing. Beginning with hands-on exposure to satellite communication systems, I have undertaken research and development projects involving LiDAR modeling, point cloud image processing algorithms, pose estimation, and the implementation of end-to-end estimation and control pipelines. These experiences have reinforced my focus on developing solutions grounded in first principles and mathematical rigor.

Beyond technical pursuits, I maintain active engagement in music, language, and finance. I hold Level 4 certification in Indian classical music from Gandharva Mahavidyalaya, and I am proficient in harmonium and flute, contributing to research on 10-finger Dhrupad flute techniques. I have also managed resources for the Neptunes Music Club and performed with the band Aaroh, representing the institute in intercollege competitions.

I am an avid enthusiast of Sanskrit, having studied it extensively during formal education and continuing to explore its literature independently. Additionally, I have contributed to financial and organizational initiatives, serving as Chief Treasurer for Dhanak 2024, the cultural festival of IIST, and as a founding member of the Money Minds Finance Club.

Across these domains, I am driven by a commitment to intellectual rigor, structured problem-solving, and the pursuit of meaningful impact in both technical and interdisciplinary endeavors.
