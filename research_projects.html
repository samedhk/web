Research Projects

Pose Estimation using Flash LiDAR

U. R. Rao Satellite Centre, ISROJan 2025 – Apr 2025

Developed and implemented a pipeline for real-time pose estimation and motion tracking in target–chaser systems using Flash LiDAR data in C++, achieving ~2 mm positional and 0.6° orientation accuracy through velocity estimation and Kalman-filter-based refinement.

Mathematical Modelling and Data Preprocessing for LiDAR

U. R. Rao Satellite Centre, ISROMay 2025 – Jul 2025

Developed comprehensive mathematical and implemented its FDTD simulation model for a Flash LiDAR system in C, along with an original data preprocessing algorithm that removes noise to enhance computational efficiency.
